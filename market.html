04. International Spot & FX Decomposition

Multiplicative accounting split of converted price movement.

Mathematical Formulation & Causation Disclaimer:

The converted INR change decomposes multiplicatively: (1 + metal USD change) × (1 + USD/INR change) − 1 = converted INR change.

Important: This is an accounting split of the theoretical converted spot price, not a claim of sole causation for domestic prices. Indian retail prices also incorporate tariffs (15% Customs Duty + 3% GST), clearing margins, and local physical demand premiums.
